<h2 id="intuition">Closing week three: never compute the same thing twice</h2>

Generating token 500 naively means running the model over all 500 preceding tokens — then token
501 runs over 501, and so on. That is enormously wasteful, because **causal masking guarantees
the earlier tokens' representations cannot change**. Token 3's key and value vectors depend only
on tokens 1–3; appending token 500 cannot affect them. So compute them once and keep them. That
is the **KV cache**, and it is the single most important inference optimization there is.

<h2 id="math">What can be reused</h2>

Attention needs a query from the *current* position and keys and values from *all* positions:

<MathBlock tex="\text{attn}(q_t, K_{1:t}, V_{1:t}) = \text{softmax}\!\left(\frac{q_t K_{1:t}^\top}{\sqrt{d_h}}\right)V_{1:t}" />

`K_{1:t-1}` and `V_{1:t-1}` are identical to what they were at the previous step. Only the new
row `k_t, v_t` is genuinely new. Caching turns per-step projection work from `O(t)` into `O(1)`,
and total generation cost from `O(n²)` into `O(n)`.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Why only K and V, not Q">
  Queries are *not* cached, because a past token's query is never needed again. Under causal
  masking, position 3 attends to positions 1–3 and that computation already happened at step 3;
  no later step revisits it. Keys and values, by contrast, are read by *every* subsequent query.
  The asymmetry is exactly why it is a "KV" cache rather than a "QKV" cache.
</Callout>

## Week three summary

Decoding is a set of choices made *after* training, and they determine output quality as much as
the weights do. Greedy is deterministic and bland; pure sampling is faithful but includes an
incoherent tail; temperature reshapes the distribution without being able to remove that tail;
top-k truncates by count and top-p by mass, with top-p adapting to the model's confidence (1
token where certain, 7 where not); penalties suppress loops at the risk of suppressing necessary
words; and the KV cache exploits causal masking to turn `O(n²)` generation into `O(n)` — a
191.6× reduction in projection work at 256 tokens, paid for in cache memory that reaches 85.90 GB
at 32k context. Week four returns to training, at the level of choosing how big a model to build.
